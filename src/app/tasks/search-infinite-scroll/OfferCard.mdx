# OfferCard

One row in the hotel search results list ([search-infinite-scroll task](/?path=/docs)). It's a
deliberately "dumb" component — it renders an `OfferWithMeta` and reports user intent
(`onToggleFavorite`, `onRefreshPrice`) up to its parent, which owns the actual favorite/price
state via `@tanstack/react-query`. That split is why it's easy to story: every variant below is
just a different `offer` object and a couple of booleans, no query client or router required.

## Price staleness

`OfferCard` doesn't know what "stale" means on its own — it's handed a `now` timestamp and
compares it against `offer.priceCheckedAt` against the exported `PRICE_STALE_AFTER_MS`
threshold. That's what makes the badge/refresh affordance easy to demonstrate here without
waiting in real time: the story just hands it a `now` far enough in the future.
